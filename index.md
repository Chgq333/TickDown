# TickDown Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

## Overview

TickDown is a Windows desktop countdown timer application developed by J-Net-Securities.

## Data Collection

TickDown does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

The application does not require user accounts and does not collect names, email addresses, location data, analytics data, or tracking information.

## Local Storage

Any application settings or preferences are stored locally on the user's device and are not transmitted to external services.

## Third-Party Services

TickDown does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.
